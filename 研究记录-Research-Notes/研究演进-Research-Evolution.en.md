# Research evolution

[Confirmed] This order follows S0's sections and S1's discussion sequence. It is an intellectual history, not a precisely timed experimental log. [Source digest](../证据-Evidence/来源摘要-Source-Digest.en.md)

## 1. From explanation credibility to acceptance

The source begins with “AI programming as alchemy” and moves toward whether code and explanations agree. [Proposal] Retain the requirement for concrete execution and checks. Strong source explanations about internal model understanding are not promoted to confirmed knowledge.

## 2. From changing tools to changing production

The electrification analogy prompts reconsideration of task units, information flow, quality control, and responsibility. [Hypothesis] Organizational changes may matter more than coding speed alone. [Open] Historical analogy cannot establish software-production benefits.

## 3. From isolation and repair to local rules

S0 reports a repair unit, state-machine swarm, and spatial fields, followed by growth, Potts, and adhesion mechanisms. [Open] This repository has not reproduced these observations; they are not completed experimental evidence here.

Preserve reported failures too: trapped enclosed regions, overpowering target fields, single-color collapse, multiple domains, and freezing without neighbor absorption. They motivate checking reversibility, conservation constraints, persistent identity, and objective design. Visual stability alone does not establish correctness.

## 4. The turn in biological analogy

Hierarchical promotion gives stable regions identities, then requires a fix for neighbor absorption. [Hypothesis] Analogies can reveal design variables, but adding more of them may shift the difficulty into hand-tuned rules. The current focus returns to organizational benefits in real software.

## 5. Four production questions

S1 identifies decomposition cost, validation cost, composition cost, and knowledge continuity. [Proposal] Make human coordination time and historical inheritance central metrics; treat repair and regeneration as alternative strategies.
